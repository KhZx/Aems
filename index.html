Features

Built for Emergency Services

Every feature designed with paramedics and supervisors in mind.

Real-Time Sync

Real-time sync through the AEMS API keeps every station updated instantly. Changes propagate across all devices within milliseconds.

Smart Alerts

Automatic notifications for expiring medication, low stock levels, and equipment requiring inspection.

Shift Reports

Generate professional shift reports with one click. Share via WhatsApp or export as PDF.

Audit Trail

Complete history of every equipment change, inspection, and usage event with timestamps.

Multi-Station

Manage equipment across all DCA stations from a single dashboard with per-station filtering.

Mobile Ready

Fully responsive design works seamlessly on tablets and smartphones used in the field.
